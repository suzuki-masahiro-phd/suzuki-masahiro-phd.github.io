Masahiro Suzuki

Postdoctoral Researcher · Chalmers University of Technology

I study how climate policies and energy transitions evolve together, and whether historical rates of change are consistent with the pace required to meet climate goals. My work focuses on political acceleration, energy-transition dynamics, and the gap between policy ambition and realized deployment.

Research

Climate policy and energy transitions

I analyse how policy interventions interact with the evolution of energy systems, with particular attention to whether policy has measurably accelerated transitions.

Political acceleration

My doctoral research examined historical interventions in the G7 and the EU and how they changed the pace and direction of energy transitions.

Targets, ambition, and delivery

I study the relationship between long-term climate targets, near-term renewable ambitions, and realized deployment, including how expectations compare with historically observed rates of change.

About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Chalmers University of Technology. I completed my PhD in Environmental Sciences and Policy at Central European University in 2024, where my dissertation examined political acceleration in energy transitions.

Before my PhD, I worked on climate-policy research at the Institute for Global Environmental Strategies and participated in international climate negotiations as a national delegate of Japan, including COP22.
